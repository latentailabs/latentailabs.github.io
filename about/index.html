About

Arun Prabhakaran

I make Latent AI Labs, a series of AI explainers built on measurement rather than metaphor.

For each episode I load an open-weight model, such as Qwen3-4B, FLUX.2, Qwen2.5-VL or Wan2.1, onto an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU, hook into its layers and record what actually happens: token IDs, attention weights, memory use, timings. The results become a long-form animated video on YouTube (@LatentAILabs) and a full written article on Medium, published in Towards AI. Claims in the articles cite the original research papers.

I also build small open models for Indian languages and publish them under Apache-2.0 on Hugging Face:

  • Yama: a zero-shot text classifier for 11 Indian languages, Hinglish and English. Read the summary.
  • Chitragupta: personal-data detection and redaction for Indian text. Read the summary.
